What is a Signal?

A signal is a measurable quantity that carries information. Examples include speech waveforms, ECG recordings, images, and radio transmissions.

Signal Classification

  • Continuous or discrete
  • Periodic or non-periodic
  • Deterministic or random
  • Energy or power
