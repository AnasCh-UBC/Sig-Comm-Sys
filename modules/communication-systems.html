Learning Outcomes

  • Explain how information moves through a communication system.
  • Identify the major components of a communication system.
  • Compare analog and digital communication.
  • Recognize communication technologies used every day.

Why Study Communication Systems?

Communication engineering is concerned with the reliable transfer of information from one location to another.

Communication systems are everywhere:

  • Smartphones
  • Wi‑Fi Networks
  • Bluetooth Devices
  • Radio Broadcasting
  • Satellite Communications
  • Streaming Media
  • Internet Services

🔵 Engineering Insight

Modern communication systems often carry voice, video, control information, location data, and Internet traffic over the same infrastructure.

Common Misconception

🟡 Information vs Signals

Information and signals are not the same thing.

Information is the message.

A signal is the physical representation of that message.

Real‑World Connection

📱 Sending a Voice Message

  • Source → Your Voice
  • Transmitter → Smartphone
  • Channel → Internet Network
  • Receiver → Recipient Device
  • Destination → Listener

Worked Example

A student records a voice message and sends it through a messaging application.
