Module 1: Communication Systems

Learning Outcomes

  • Describe the communication process
  • Identify source, transmitter, channel and receiver
  • Compare analog and digital systems

Why Study Communication Systems?

Every phone call, Wi‑Fi connection, radio broadcast and streaming service relies on a communication system that transfers information from a source to a destination.

The Communication Chain

  1. Source: Produces information.
  2. Transmitter: Converts information into a signal.
  3. Channel: Carries the signal.
  4. Receiver: Recovers the information.
  5. Destination: End user.

Knowledge Check

In a cellphone call, what is the channel?

AnswerThe wireless radio link between devices and network infrastructure.

Summary

  • Communication systems move information.
  • Signals are representations of information.
  • Channels introduce impairments and noise.
